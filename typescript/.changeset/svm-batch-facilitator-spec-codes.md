The SVM batch-settlement facilitator reports the spec error codes for setup transactions and the request envelope. A deposit whose `open` or `top_up` transaction fails the sponsor checks (layout, signers, compute budget, size, decoding) is now `invalid_batch_settlement_svm_setup_transaction` instead of `transaction_failed`; a binding Memo naming another receiver authorizer is `invalid_batch_settlement_svm_receiver_authorizer_mismatch`, a grace period other than `extra.withdrawDelay` is `invalid_batch_settlement_svm_withdraw_delay_mismatch`, and an unaccepted message version is `unsupported_transaction_version`. A deposit proof the escrow cannot cover is `invalid_batch_settlement_svm_cumulative_exceeds_deposit`, kept apart from `invalid_batch_settlement_svm_cumulative_amount_mismatch` for a voucher that does not match server state. A refund of a sealed or distributed channel, or one whose voucher lies outside the settled-to-deposit range, is `invalid_batch_settlement_svm_close_state`. The batch server classifies its own setup-transaction failures the same way. `/verify` now requires `paymentPayload.accepted` to equal `paymentRequirements` in every field except the per-402 `recentBlockhash` and `recentSlot`, and reports a mismatch (including a network mismatch) as `invalid_payment_requirements` instead of `network_mismatch`.
