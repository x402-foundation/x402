**Breaking:** EVM client RPC is configured once, on the signer. `toClientEvmSigner(account, rpc)` accepts a viem public client, `{ rpcUrl }` for every network, or `{ [chainId]: { rpcUrl } }`, and the composed `readContract(args, network)`, `getTransactionCount(args, network)` and `estimateFeesPerGas(undefined, network)` route by the trailing CAIP-2 network; a signer's own reads take precedence and implementations that ignore the argument keep compiling. A public client bound to another chain, or a chain-id map without the network, throws the new `EvmRpcNotConfiguredError` instead of reading. Removed: the options argument of the `ExactEvmScheme` and `UptoEvmScheme` clients, `schemeOptions` on `registerExactEvmScheme`, `rpcUrl` on `BatchSettlementEvmSchemeOptions`, and the client types `ExactEvmSchemeConfig`, `ExactEvmSchemeConfigByChainId`, `ExactEvmSchemeOptions`, `UptoEvmSchemeConfig`, `UptoEvmSchemeConfigByChainId` and `UptoEvmSchemeOptions` (the facilitator configs of the same name are unchanged); `readChannelBalanceAndTotalClaimed` now takes the channel network. Migrate `new ExactEvmScheme(account, { rpcUrl })` to `new ExactEvmScheme(toClientEvmSigner(account, { rpcUrl }))`. When the signer cannot read a network, gas sponsoring still warns once per network and pays without the sponsored permit or approval, and batch-settlement falls back to local channel state. New `assertClientEvmCapabilities(signer, capabilities, label)` throws `"<label> requires <capability> on the signer."`. Unchanged on the wire.
