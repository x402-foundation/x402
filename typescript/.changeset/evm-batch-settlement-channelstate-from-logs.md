The batch-settlement facilitator now derives the deposit and refund settle `extra.channelState` from the confirmed receipt's `Deposited`, `Claimed` and `Refunded` events plus the pre-transaction state, instead of polling channel state after confirmation. A deposit receipt without a `Deposited` event for the channel fails with `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_deposit_transaction_failed`, and a receipt whose `logs` field is missing fails with the new `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_receipt_logs_unavailable`; both are terminal. A failed pre-refund channel read now rejects with `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_rpc_read_failed` before broadcast. The refund `withdrawRequestedAt` now survives a partial withdrawal reduction and `totalClaimed` comes from the bundled `Claimed` event. Voucher verify re-reads channel state after each `CHANNEL_STATE_REREAD_DELAYS_MS` wait (at most three re-reads) while the balance cannot cover `maxClaimableAmount`, and the server deposit and refund after-settle hooks never lower a stored `totalClaimed` or `refundNonce`. The deposit hook also never lowers `withdrawRequestedAt`; a refund snapshot of 0 still clears it. An omitted `channelState` field, including a missing `channelState` object, keeps the stored value and does not reject the settle. A present field that is not a canonical non-negative integer rejects with `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_refund_payload`. A reverted deposit, refund, claim, or settle receipt reports `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_transaction_reverted`; the operation-specific transaction-failed codes stay for a bad broadcast hash or a receipt that is missing its expected event. A deposit reconcile whose follow-up channel read fails still returns `channelState.balance` from `Deposited.newBalance` and omits the fields that event does not carry. A refund uses a same-receipt `Deposited.newBalance` and subtracts one following `Refunded`. A same-receipt `ChannelClosed` reports balance, `totalClaimed` and `withdrawRequestedAt` as 0. Otherwise balance is the pre-read minus `Refunded.amount`, and `balance` is omitted when that is negative, when the pre-read showed a pending withdrawal, or when the pre-read is older than half the channel's `withdrawDelay`, since `finalizeWithdraw` can lower the balance without a refund event. When a deposit receipt has more than one `Deposited` event for the same channel and amount, `channelState.balance` is the last event's `newBalance`. The refund after-settle hook deletes a channel only when that response includes a balance at or under the charged total. A refund whose `channelState` omits `balance` sets `onchainSyncedAt` to 0, so the next voucher re-reads instead of trusting the pre-refund balance. A deposit that omits `totalClaimed` or `withdrawRequestedAt` leaves `onchainSyncedAt` unchanged. A delegated refund is signed only after the pre-read succeeds and the refundable amount is not zero. `BatchSettlementEvmScheme` (facilitator) now takes a `BatchSettlementFacilitatorEvmSigner`, whose `waitForTransactionReceipt` returns required `logs`; this is type-only, signers built with `toFacilitatorEvmSigner` over a viem client already conform, and a custom signer that declares `logs` optional must make it required. The `channelState` wire shape is unchanged.
