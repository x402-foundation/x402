Keep the batch-settlement redemption worker going when the facilitator refuses one channel. A facilitator answer that refuses a request, returned as `success: false` or thrown as a `SettleError` (such as an HTTP 403 from a facilitator policy hook), no longer ends the pass: a claim or distribute batch refused outright is retried one channel at a time, so the other channels in it are still claimed and paid out, a channel still refused is submitted alone in later passes, and a refused `seal` no longer skips the rest of the pass. Failures that may still land or pass on their own (`settlement_pending`, `duplicate_settlement`, a response naming a transaction, or a 5xx, 408, 425 or 429) are not split, and the same channels are sent together again on later passes until the facilitator answers for good. Each retry is rebuilt from the current vouchers, so a channel's newer voucher makes it a new request to the facilitator; the onchain watermark keeps the cost of a second broadcast to one failed transaction. When a later claim or distribute is answered `cumulative_amount_mismatch` because an earlier request landed unseen, the worker records the confirmed watermarks of the channels the chain shows already claimed or paid out and claims the rest again. These answers are reported to `onError` and no longer reject `redeem()`; a request that gets no answer (a timeout or network error) still ends the pass as before.
