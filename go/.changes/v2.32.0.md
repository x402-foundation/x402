### Changed
- '**Breaking:** SVM batch-settlement facilitator `/settle` now costs about two storage operations instead of seven to fifteen and succeeds only when the call broadcast and confirmed the transaction itself or finished a `settlement_pending` record found at its start. `batch-settlement/facilitator.Config` takes `PendingSettlementStore x402.PendingSettlementStore` (unset defaults to a process-local store that keeps entries for `PendingRetention`, 24 hours) and the new `DuplicateSettlementStore svm.DuplicateSettlementStore` (`Reserve`/`Release`, in-memory default `svm.NewInMemoryDuplicateSettlementStore`) in place of the batch-local `PendingSettlementStore`, `ConditionalPendingStore` and `InMemoryPendingSettlementStore`, which are removed together with `CompletedBroadcastSuffix`. The facilitator reserves the signed signature before sending, so a concurrent duplicate answers `duplicate_settlement` instead of broadcasting or sharing the holder''s success, and writes the pending store only when a confirmation times out. Top-up and `request_close` keys use the transaction message hash. A claim at or below the onchain `settled` watermark fails with `cumulative_amount_mismatch`, and a cooperative refund or `request_close` retry on a `Closing` channel fails with `close_state`. A top-up retry reconciles its pending signature before it is simulated, so a top-up that landed after a timeout is answered with its original signature. A retry of a completed `seal`, refund or `distribute` fails with `close_state` or `cumulative_amount_mismatch` and carries no transaction signature. A distribute keeps its pending record per channel set and finishes an unfinished payout before starting another pass, also after a later claim moved the watermarks or rent cleanup reclaimed the channel; only its duplicate reservation follows the `settled` watermark. With `OnDistributionConfirmed` set, the payout record is written once the transaction confirms and deleted after the hook has run; a retry that finds it runs the hook again, deletes it and returns the payout, and a failed hook or delete answers `settlement_pending` and keeps the record, so the hook runs at least once for every payout whose confirmation was observed or reconciled and must be idempotent per transaction; a payout that confirms after the process stopped without observing it does not reach the hook. The in-memory default stores check only the requested entry''s expiry and sweep the rest at most once a minute. The timeout record carries the signed transaction and its blockhash: a retry sends the kept transaction again while it can still land, and drops it once its blockhash expired without landing, then broadcasts afresh. A retry of a deposit whose open timed out and has since landed is answered, like the exact schemes, only from its pending signature: when the channel holds exactly the open''s deposit with nothing settled, the deposit is the payer-signed open of that channel and a pending signature is recorded, it is reconciled and cleared once, for a delegated channel only for the caller that opened it; without a record the deposit is judged as a top-up and rejected as before. Wire format unchanged.' ([#4222](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/pull/4222)) - Thanks [@phdargen](https://github.com/phdargen) and [@cursoragent](https://github.com/cursoragent)!

- 'SVM batch-settlement `BatchChannelManager` no longer resubmits a claim, distribute or seal forever when the transaction confirmed but its response was lost. The facilitator answers a claim at or below the onchain `settled` watermark (`cumulative_amount_mismatch`), a distribute with nothing left to sweep (`cumulative_amount_mismatch`) and a seal on a channel that is no longer `Closing` or with a voucher below `settled` (`close_state`) as a `SettleResponse` carrying `extra.channelStates[]` of `{channelId, status, balance, totalClaimed, payoutWatermark, withdrawRequestedAt}`, and a successful `settle` carries it too, also in the stored payout result a retry replays. The server adopts the entries only when the response is bound to its network and names exactly the submitted channels with `payoutWatermark <= totalClaimed <= balance`: a claim batch resyncs the channels already claimed and re-claims the rest once, a distribute adopts `payoutWatermark`, and a seal records a `distributed` channel as paid out or a `sealed` one by its `settled` value, none of them counted as new claims or seals. On success without a facilitator state the server infers `Settled = SignedMaxClaimable` after a claim without `extra.accepts` and `PayoutWatermark = Settled` after a distribute. **Breaking:** the manager reads the chain only through a configured `ReadSettledWatermark`, `ReadPayoutWatermark` or `RPCURL` and no longer falls back to the network''s public RPC; a deployment that relied on that fallback sets `RPCURL`. Wire format is additive: the new `extra.channelStates` field is ignored by older servers, which keep their previous behavior.' ([#4222](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/pull/4222)) - Thanks [@phdargen](https://github.com/phdargen) and [@cursoragent](https://github.com/cursoragent)!
- Exact EVM amounts that are not uint256 decimal strings are rejected. The server names the amount when building requirements, after converting a dotted human amount. Verify and settle return invalid_exact_evm_required_amount for a leading plus, which integer parsing previously accepted as the same integer. ([#4304](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/pull/4304)) - Thanks [@PhilBot402](https://github.com/PhilBot402) and [@phdargen](https://github.com/phdargen)!
- 'SVM batch-settlement `Settle` now checks the payment envelope like the upto schemes and EVM batch-settlement: `paymentPayload.accepted` must carry the `batch-settlement` scheme and the requirements'' network. The amount may still differ, so settlement overrides keep charging below the accepted ceiling. Wire format unchanged.' - 'EVM batch-settlement refunds record a broadcast transaction in the `PendingSettlementStore` when the receipt wait fails, as deposits already do. A retry of the same refund (same channel and refund nonce, whatever amount it recomputes) waits on that transaction instead of re-simulating it, so a refund that already landed no longer fails with `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_refund_simulation_failed` or `refund_no_balance`, and a direct-mode retry no longer refunds twice. The retry stays `settlement_pending` until the transaction confirms and the advanced refund nonce is visible onchain; a reverted one is terminal. `SubmitContext` gains an optional `PendingStore`; nil keeps the previous behavior.'
