The batch-settlement EVM facilitator now rejects malformed deposit, voucher, refund, claim and settle payloads (missing or wrongly typed fields, invalid addresses, channel ids or salts, non-hex voucher, claim or authorizer signatures, numeric fields that are not uint256 decimal strings or, for claim amounts, above uint128, a `withdrawDelay` outside uint40) with the payload type's reason, such as `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_deposit_payload`, instead of raising. `invalid_message` / `error_message` names the malformed field.
